The process we are building

01Apply with clear permission to review a PDF resume and receive an on-screen application ID after a successful upload.
02Take a role- and experience-specific practical check. Anti-spam controls flag risk without claiming to prove identity.
03AI may help organize evidence, but a human reviews skills and decides whether a role-specific Verified badge is earned.
04Show only current employer-approved jobs. Candidates choose if their profile is shared with an employer.
